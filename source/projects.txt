# Ongoing Projects

This is a list of projects I'm currently working on, and projects I've worked on in the past. It's not comprehensive. I started it on November 22, 2026, when I felt a burst of inspiration hit me while surfing around [Gemini | https://geminiprotocol.net]. Isn't it great when that happens? It makes me feel so alive.

## Stuff I am currently working on

This is stuff I'm actively working on. A link might be provided with each project, should they be public; otherwise it means they're either private or that they haven't reached enough maturity to be published yet.

* **[Eterspire | https://www.eterspire.com]**, an MMORPG and the best thing I've ever built. This is also my main job.
* **[LartuNet | home]**, my personal website (this very site!). I actively maintain it using Makompile (see below), especially this list and the [book club | bookclub] one. From time to time I change the way it looks and behaves, but it is still my personal corner of the internet.
* **PolyComposer**, name tbd though. A graphical engine for making games for modern and old systems alike. At the moment it supports modern and classic macOS, PSP, PlayStation 2, web, iOS and Android.
* **Axonote**, name subject to change. A Telegram Saved Messages-inspired note taking app for iOS and macOS.
