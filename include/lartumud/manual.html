This is a placeholder manual. Eterspire Beta 1 used to have a lovely manual explaying how to play the game in full detail, but I have yet to migrate it here. It may happen, it may not. Don't wait up.

  • Type /controls in the chatbox to learn the controls.
  • Type /help to check all the chat commands.
  • To talk to NPCs, move close to them and say hi. Their speech balloon icon will turn green when you are close enough.
  • Sitting helps you heal faster.
